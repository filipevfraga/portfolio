Featured project: AI receptionist for a beauty clinic
Result: over 690 conversations handled with zero failures.
The problem: the clinic lost patients because messages went unanswered, especially outside business hours, and staff spent hours repeating the same answers.
What I built: an AI receptionist that works 24/7 on WhatsApp.
• Answers patient questions in a warm, natural tone
• Looks up procedures and prices, even with misspelled or popular names
• Manages appointments and keeps a copy of the clinic's calendar in sync
• Saves after-hours requests and delivers them to staff at 7:30 every morning
• Sends instant WhatsApp alerts if anything fails
Stack: n8n, OpenAI API, WhatsApp (Evolution API), Airtable, Redis, webhooks
How it works:
1. A patient sends a WhatsApp message.
2. n8n receives it through a webhook and loads the conversation memory.
3. The AI agent checks procedures in Airtable and the clinic's schedule.
4. It replies, books or hands the conversation to a human when needed.
5. Errors trigger an alert to me by WhatsApp.
